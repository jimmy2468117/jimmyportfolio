2022 — TRB.inc Mini Campfire Pit

Portfolio – Product Design / Engineering

Commissioned to develop an outdoor campfire pit centered on the core requirements of durability, easy storage, and cost control.

From the early design stage, “maximum material utilization” was set as the primary objective, treating structure, nesting layout, and manufacturing process as a single integrated design problem.

The main body, grill mesh, and bottom wind shield are all cut from a single 1mm stainless steel sheet, achieving near-zero material waste through highly efficient nesting.

Underwent multiple structural iterations and real-fire tests to ensure strength, combustion efficiency, and a satisfying assembly experience.

Highlights:
・Zero-waste nesting from a single steel sheet
・15-second tool-free assembly, no screws
・Self-locking structural design

Design & Production Location: Taiwan
Material: Stainless Steel

Date: 2022
