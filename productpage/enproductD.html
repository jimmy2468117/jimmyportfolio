Chien Hui .inc Filter Tip Mold Reconstruction

Portfolio – Industrial Engineering

Took over a long-used filter tip steel mold with missing original drawings and severe wear. In a very short timeframe, completed disassembly, 3D scanning, reverse engineering of design intent, draft angle correction, and rebuilt a complete set of modern CNC-machinable drawings. The new mold achieved mass-production quality right from the T0 trial run and has remained in stable production ever since.

Focus:
Reverse Engineering, Mold Restoration, Mass Production Readiness

Date: 2024
