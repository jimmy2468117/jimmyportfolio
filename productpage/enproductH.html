2021 — TRB.inc Outdoor Camping Knife

Portfolio – Product Design

Designed from real-world outdoor usage scenarios, this is a sheepsfoot-style camping knife focused on precision tasks.

The blade profile is optimized for common camping needs such as whittling wood, digging, and light prying, while prioritizing grip comfort, safety, and fatigue-free performance during extended use.

Handled the complete form design, steel selection (440C), heat treatment specification, handle proportioning, and surface finish decisions. Worked closely with the manufacturing partner through multiple rounds of refinement to perfect mass-production details.

Design & Production Location: Taiwan
Material: 440C Stainless Steel

Date: 2021
