TRB.inc Tactical Fastpac

Portfolio – Product Design

Co-developed with Freestone. Planned the appearance, structure, pattern making, and mass production details.

Uses MIT 600D DWR+PU fabric, YKK waterproof zippers, and Duraflex hardware.

This is a small crossbody bag featuring a full front MOLLE system panel, blending tactical and "metro wilderness" elements.

It maintains a sleek, minimalist look while incorporating full functionality and an unrestricted, versatile tactical style.
