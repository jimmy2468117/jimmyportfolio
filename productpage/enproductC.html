2025 — Articulated Building Block Figure Development

Portfolio – Product Design / Engineering

Commissioned to participate in the design and mass-production integration of an articulated building-block-style toy.

Primarily responsible for character proportion setting, articulation structure planning, joint engineering optimization, 3D modeling, mold handover coordination, and mass-production support.

In the early stages of the project, the team faced challenges with structural durability and joint configuration. By reorganizing the engineering logic and aligning it with manufacturing constraints, the product was successfully advanced to the mass-production phase.

Design & Production Location: Taiwan
Material: ABS

Date: 2025
